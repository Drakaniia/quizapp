Tapeworms

Tapeworms are parasites of vertebrates and lack a digestive system: Tapeworms absorb nutrients from the host's intestine. Fertilized eggs, produced by sexual reproduction, leave the host's body in feces.

Rotifers

Rotifers, phylum Rotifera, are tiny animals that inhabit fresh water; the ocean, and damp soil: Rotifers are smaller than many protists but are truly multicellular and have specialized organ systems.
