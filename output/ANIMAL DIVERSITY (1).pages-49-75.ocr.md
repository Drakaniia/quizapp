Arthropods

Two out of every three known species of animals are arthropods. Members of the phylum Arthropoda are found in nearly all habitats of the biosphere. The arthropod body plan consists of a segmented body, hard exoskeleton, and jointed appendages, and dates to the Cambrian explosion (535-525 million years ago).
